## Overview

Costco Wholesale Corporation operates membership warehouses and e-commerce sites globally, focusing on offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products to drive high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover. As of August 30, 2026, the Company operated 939 warehouses worldwide, with an average warehouse size of approximately 148,000 square feet, designed for efficient selling space and inventory control [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 L000733-L000742]. Costco purchases most merchandise directly from suppliers and uses cross-docking depots to consolidate shipments before distribution, thereby lowering distribution costs [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 L000738-L000741]. The core merchandise selection per warehouse is limited to less than 4,000 SKUs to focus on fast-selling items [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 L000744]. Membership at the end of fiscal 2026 was 84.1 million paid members worldwide, with a renewal rate of 92.3% in the U.S. and Canada [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 L000776-L000783]. Ancillary services including gasoline stations, which operated 774 stations, represented 11% of total net sales [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 L000764].

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Costco positions itself as a low-price, high-volume retailer with a strong membership model emphasizing operational efficiencies achieved through a streamlined warehouse format and controlled SKU assortment. The limited SKU approach, carrying less than 4,000 active SKUs per warehouse, supports cost control and rapid inventory turnover [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 L000744]. The membership model contributes significantly to revenue and profitability, with Executive members representing 42.3 million of the 84.1 million paid members and accounting for approximately 75.2% of worldwide net sales in fiscal 2026 [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 L000782]. The Company continues geographic expansion in multiple countries and expansion of its e-commerce platform, which achieved a digitally-enabled comparable sales growth of 20.9% in fiscal 2026 [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 L000734, L000767; EX-99.1 accession 0000909832-26-000084 L000018]. Risks to growth include competition for suitable warehouse locations, local regulatory and community restrictions, and sales cannibalization from new openings [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 L000839-L000841].
