## Overview

Costco Wholesale Corporation operates 939 membership warehouses and e-commerce sites primarily in the U.S., Canada, Mexico, Japan, the U.K., Korea, Australia, Taiwan, China, Spain, France, Sweden, Iceland, and New Zealand [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line L000734]. Its business model centers on offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally branded and private-label products, focusing on high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line L000737]. Costco buys most merchandise directly from suppliers, routing shipments via cross-docking depots or directly to warehouses to maximize distribution efficiency [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 lines L000738-L000741]. Warehouses average approximately 148,000 square feet and emphasize operational efficiency over presentation [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line L000742]. Each warehouse carries less than 4,000 SKUs in physical locations, with an online assortment of 9,000 to 11,000 SKUs [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line L000744]. Ancillary services include gasoline stations, pharmacy, optical, and travel. Membership is fundamental, with a $65 annual fee in the U.S. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 lines L000773, L000782]. Costco had 84,100 thousand paid members and 42,300 thousand executive members as of 2026, with executive members generating 75.2% of worldwide net sales [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 lines L000776, L000782]. The company employed 355,000 people worldwide, mostly in warehouses and distribution, with about 5% unionized [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 lines L000789-L000790].

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Costco’s competitive positioning is based on low-price leadership on a curated product mix, high inventory turnover, and operational efficiencies in distribution and labor, supported by a strong membership model that provides stable fee revenue and drives repeat visits [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line L000737]. The private-label merchandise enhances differentiation by focusing on quality and value [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line L000768]. Geographic diversification encompasses North America, Asia, and Europe [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line L000734; EX-99.1 accession 0000909832-26-000084 lines L000021]. Net sales increased 11.2% in Q4 2026 and 10.1% in FY 2026 to $93.9 billion and $297.2 billion respectively [EX-99.1 accession 0000909832-26-000084 line L000010]. Comparable sales growth was 8.2% in the U.S., 7.8% in Canada, and 9.8% in other international markets for FY 2026 [EX-99.1 accession 0000909832-26-000084 line L000016]. Digitally-enabled sales grew 20.9%, indicating e-commerce as a key growth channel [EX-99.1 accession 0000909832-26-000084 line L000018]. The gasoline ancillary business contributed 11% of total net sales [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line L000764]. Customer concentration risk is notable, with U.S. and Canadian operations comprising 86% of net sales and California alone accounting for 26% of U.S. sales [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 lines L000836-L000837]. Expansion plans include new warehouses in existing and new markets but face real estate, regulatory, and competitive challenges [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 lines L000839-L000841].

## Future Developments and Strategic Recommendations

Costco is focused on expanding its warehouse footprint (939 warehouses as of August 2026) and scaling its e-commerce capabilities to drive growth [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line L000734]. Management should continue prioritizing operational efficiency and member satisfaction to uphold high renewal rates of 92.3% in the U.S. and Canada [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line L000783]. Addressing geographic concentration and regulatory risks, especially exposure to the California market, is critical due to its material contribution to revenue and operating income [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line L000836]. Accelerating investment in digital platforms is advised to exploit faster digitally-enabled sales growth [EX-99.1 accession 0000909832-26-000084 line L000018]. Monitoring cannibalization risks from new warehouse openings on existing stores is necessary to sustain profitability [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line L000840]. Active cost controls related to labor and wages are prudent given potential inflationary pressures [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line L000787]. Supply chain diversification remains important to mitigate risks from supplier dependencies or geopolitical issues [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line L000768].
